Match details

Match clock

Tap a marker at that moment in the video. Footage before the throw-in, during half-time and after full-time is left out of the stats, and events show in match time. Ends swap at half-time. In extra time, the first period uses the 1st-half ends and the second uses the 2nd-half ends.

Events

All events

Tap an event to jump to 5 seconds before it, or to its saved drawing. Use the event filter to pick one or more events, for example kickouts won and kickouts lost. The list, pitch map, timeline and reel all follow it. Save reel keeps the current filter under a name, so it fills up as you tag more events. Export reel records it to a video file with a caption on each clip; keep this tab open while it runs. The reel plays every event in the list, one after another, and holds for 4 seconds on any saved drawing.

Pitch map

Tag an event, then tap the pitch to place it. For a kickout, tap where it landed: the app draws the arrow from the kicking team’s goal and works out short or long and the wing. Shape and colour show the event, and the ring is the team’s colour. Tap keys to show one or more events. The map also follows the filters above. The solid arc is the D and the dashed arc is the 40m two-point arc. Filter to kickouts to see the 52m arc that splits short from long.

Kickout and turnover breakdown (total)

A turnover leads to a shot or score when the team that won it shoots before losing the ball again (a kickout, a turnover, a free to the other team or the end of the half). Frees they win in between don’t break the link. A turnover lost by one team counts as won by the other, so you only need to tag it once. If you tag it from both sides within 3 seconds, it’s counted once. Frees work the same way.
